# **Sai Motor Works Online Booking — Proposal & Feature Sign-off**

Nov 1, 2026 · Prepared by @Naman

## **Overview**

Your existing check-in and jobs system gets online booking from your website and automatic SMS to customers, for a one-time **\$600** plus SMS charged by usage.

Today bookings come by phone or walk-in and go into a basic calendar. Customers get no confirmation, no reminder and no "your car is ready" message. The booking add-on fixes that:

* Customers book a drop-off day online, any time, without phoning.  
* Customers get an SMS when they book, a reminder the day before, and an SMS when the car is ready.  
* Phone bookings go straight into the same system from your phone, so none are forgotten.

Please read this document, tick or change the items in **How it will work**, and sign at the end. Anything you change will be built that way.

## **What's included in version 1**

Version 1 covers online booking, automatic booking SMS, a one-tap "car ready" SMS, a Bookings tab in your dashboard, and owner settings.

| Area | What you get |
| :---- | :---- |
| Online booking page | A booking page opened from a "Book now" button on your website. Customers pick services and a drop-off day, then enter their car and contact details. Works on phones. No customer account needed. |
| Live availability | Only days with free spots are shown. Two customers can never take the last spot on the same day. |
| SMS to customers | 1\. Booking confirmed (straight away). 2\. Reminder the day before. 3\. Car ready for pickup, sent when you press **Send ready SMS** (works for booked cars and walk-ins). Plus a short SMS if a booking is cancelled or moved. Sent under your name, e.g. "SaiMotor". |
| Customer self-service | The SMS includes a private link so customers can view, cancel or move their booking, up to a cut-off you choose. |
| Bookings on your dashboard | A new Bookings tab in your existing dashboard: today's and upcoming bookings, notes, and SMS delivery status. You get a notification for each new online booking, like you do for check-ins. |
| Phone bookings | Add a phone booking from your phone in about 30 seconds while you are on the call, so nothing has to be entered later. Returning customers fill in from their mobile number. You can go over the daily limit when needed. Or send the customer a booking link by SMS so they can book themselves. |
| Joins up with check-in and jobs | On the day, the customer checks in on the front iPad as now. The check-in is matched to their booking by rego or mobile, and the job sheet links to it as it does today. |
| Booking settings | Services and prices, booking days and hours, cars per day, closed days and holidays, cut-off times, SMS sender name, and whether the ready SMS is button-only or automatic. Owner only. |
| Customer history | Previous no-shows are shown on new bookings. |

## **Not included in version 1**

These can be added later and would be quoted separately:

* Invoicing, quotes and taking payments online  
* Parts and inventory  
* Photos and customer signatures at check-in  
* Marketing SMS or email (e.g. service-due reminders months later)  
* Customer accounts and logins  
* A phone app (it runs in the web browser on phones, iPads and computers)  
* Connecting to or importing from your current calendar software

| Ongoing hosting and support | \[Included / \$X per month\] | \[— / Monthly\] |
| Changes or new features | Quoted before any work starts | As agreed |

**The \$600 covers:**

* Adding booking to your existing system and setting up your services, prices, hours and cars per day  
* Everything in "What's included in version 1"  
* Help adding the "Book now" button to your website  
* A training session for you and your mechanics  
* Fixing any faults found in the first \[30\] days after go-live, at no charge

**How SMS is counted:** one SMS is up to 160 characters. Longer messages count as 2\. The confirmation SMS includes the booking link, so it is usually 2\.

Example: 100 bookings a month × 3 messages each is 300 messages, about 400 SMS once the longer confirmations count as 2\. At an example rate of 10c per SMS, that is about \$40 a month. You can see your SMS count at any time.

## **Timeline**

Online booking can be ready for a trial about **7 days after you confirm the rules above**, then go live after a short test.

1. **You review and sign this document.** Any changes go into the build.  
2. **Build (about 7 days).** Booking is built into your existing system and set up with your services and hours.  
3. **Test (2–3 days).** You and your mechanics try it with test bookings. No SMS goes to real customers.  
4. **Go live.** The "Book now" button goes on your website and real bookings start.  
5. **First \[30\] days.** We check in weekly and fix anything that isn't working right.
